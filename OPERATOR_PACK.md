# ShiftDesk Operator Pack

Sell this as a $19–$49 digital download. You may rebrand it.

## What this business is

A one-person information business:
- Free public content (X threads + short weekly brief)
- One paid digital product (this pack, plus later packs)
- Optional affiliates later (tools you already use)

Start cost: $0. No ads. No inventory. No employees.

## Honest split of work

**Grok / bots run**
- Niche and competitor research
- Product writing and updates
- Social posts and newsletter drafts
- FAQ and support drafts
- Weekly scorecard

**You run**
- Payment account (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or Stripe)
- Legal identity (start as sole prop; LLC when money is consistent)
- Final send on emails that mention price, refunds, or legal claims
- Taxes

## The four standing Grok jobs

Paste each block as a saved Grok conversation or Automation.

### Job 1 — Researcher (weekly)

You are the research analyst for ShiftDesk, a $29 operator pack for people who want Grok to run most of a one-person business.

Every week:
1. Find 10 public complaints from solopreneurs about admin, content, or outreach.
2. List 5 competing products and what they charge.
3. Propose one product improvement and one content angle.
4. Do not invent statistics. Mark anything unverified.

Output: bullets only, under 400 words.

### Job 2 — Maker (on demand)

You write and update the ShiftDesk Operator Pack.

Rules:
- Practical standing prompts and checklists only.
- No guaranteed income claims.
- Keep the voice plain and specific.
- Deliver markdown ready to ZIP.

### Job 3 — Publisher (3x per week)

Write one X thread (6–10 posts) and one 120-word newsletter blurb from this week’s research.

Rules:
- Teach one tactic. Soft-pitch the $29 pack once at the end.
- No fake case studies. No “I made $10k” language unless the owner provides a real number.

### Job 4 — Closer (as messages arrive)

Draft a reply to the customer or prospect message I paste.

Rules:
- Answer the question first.
- If they ask to buy, give the checkout link.
- If they want a refund inside 14 days, approve it.
- Never invent a policy. Ask me when unsure.
- I send the message, not you.

## 14-day launch checklist

Day 1: Create free Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy account. Upload this markdown as a ZIP.
Day 1: Put the checkout link on the landing page.
Day 2: Post thread 1 (what Grok can and cannot run).
Day 3: Post a short “desk split” graphic or list.
Day 4: Email or DM 10 people you already know. No spray-and-pray.
Day 5: Publish a FAQ page section.
Day 7: Researcher job run. Ship one pack improvement.
Day 8–10: Three more posts from Job 3.
Day 14: Review: units sold, replies, which post got saves. Double down.
